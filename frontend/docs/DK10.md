# DK10 — Supply & demand (done)

Progress: DK0 ✔ … DK10 ✔ → next DK11 (price action). Gate G1 (use DK7 export on a real day) still open.

Deviations:
- Departure = shortest same-direction run ending at a bar (latest start, ≤ `dispWithinBars` bars) with net move ≥ `sdDispAtr` × ATR (ATR of the bar before the run). `known_at` = close of the run's last bar. Band, direction and grade are frozen then.
- Base = up to `baseBarsMax` contiguous bars immediately before the departure whose **body** ≤ `baseAtr` × ATR (ATR before the departure). Body, not range: the default 0.8 × ATR cannot hold a bar's full range. Zero-body bars qualify, so a flat stretch directly before a base extends it (max 6). No qualifying base ⇒ no S/D zone from that departure.
- Bounds: `body` (default; falls back to wick when the body band has no height) or `wick`. Demand = bull departure, supply = bear departure; distal edge = far edge from price, proximal = near edge.
- Order block = the candle right before the departure when it is the opposite colour. OB band overlapping the base band > `obMergeOverlap` (intersection ÷ smaller height) ⇒ one zone tagged OB (`order_block` factor, `Demand zone · OB`); otherwise a separate OB zone. With no qualifying base the OB is emitted alone (`Bullish order block`, `base` factor opposes). Type stays `sd_zone`; the adapter uses role `order_block` for OB-tagged zones, `premium_discount` otherwise. No new colours.
- Lifecycle: `fresh` → `partially_mitigated` (wick enters the proximal edge, `depth` 0–1) → `fully_mitigated` (wick reaches the midpoint) → `invalidated` (close beyond the distal edge, terminal). `tested` is not emitted. Each fact is known at its bar close; they may share a bar.
- Validity checklist = grade factors: `base`, `departure`, `imbalance` (only if the departure completed a 3-bar gap by `known_at`), `order_block`, `tf`, and `location` = unavailable until DK11 (premium/discount).
- `origin_id` = the kernel displacement covering the departure (started within it, ended by its last bar), so zones share it with FVGs → `shared_origin` relation.
- New default `supplyDemand.fullDepartAtr` 4.0 (rulesVersion changes). Heuristic significance = TF weight × min(1, departure ATR ÷ `fullDepartAtr`).
- Harness: prefix-equality on all 5 fixtures, a lifecycle walk (bull + mirrored), deep H1 and deep M15. 5,000 bars: M15 6.7 ms, M1 8.2 ms (detector only).
