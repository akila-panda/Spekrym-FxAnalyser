# DK11 — Price action (done)

Progress: DK0 ✔ … DK11 ✔ → next DK12 (confluence & MTF relations). Gate G1 (use DK7 export on a real day) still open.

Deviations:
- One `priceAction` detector (registered after `sd`, before `sample`) emits impulses, ranges, trendlines and candle signals. `Detector.run` gains an optional third argument `prior` (objects from earlier-registered detectors, read-only) — only candle signals use it.
- Ranges (swing scale): 2 consecutive equal swing highs + 2 consecutive equal swing lows within `rangeAtr` × ATR, height ≥ `rangeMinAtr` × ATR, no close beyond an edge (± tolerance) since the first swing. Known at the 4th swing's confirmation; band frozen; a repeat at the same edges while unbroken is absorbed. Lifecycle: `active` → `broken` (close beyond an edge by `breakAtr` × ATR, `dir` up/down) → `retested` and/or `failed_breakout` (close back inside) within `failBars`. First breakout only.
- Trend/range class: `marketClass(k, cfg)` = intact range → `range`, else structure trend (`trend_up` / `trend_down` / `transitioning`). A helper for the brief (DK13), not an object.
- Trendlines (swing scale): rising support through swing lows, falling resistance through swing highs; two consecutive-touch anchors + later touches within `tlAtr` × ATR until `tlMinTouches`; no swing or close beyond the line. Known at the last required touch's confirmation; frozen; `broken` = close beyond by the tolerance. All valid lines are emitted; "best `tlKeepPerTf` per TF and side" is a view-time pick, `bestTrendlines` (a causal detector cannot rank by touches not yet known). Not yet wired into `selectForView`. Overlapping lines from successive touch pairs are not merged.
- Impulses = kernel displacements with the same ids as the sample detector's (these win the de-dupe). Lifecycle: `impulse` → `correction` (pullback ≥ `corrAtr` × ATR from the running extreme, first time) → `invalidated` (close beyond the origin).
- Candle signals (engulfing, pin bar) exist only within location: the tip (low for bullish, high for bearish) within `locAtr` × ATR of a prior level / zone / pool / FVG already known and not spent at the signal bar's close. Pin = wick ≥ `pinWickFrac` of the bar range; both need range ≥ `signalMinRangeAtr` × ATR. Direction is the pattern's, not a forecast.
- Premium/discount: `confirmedRange(k, t)` = latest confirmed swing-scale high and low (50% equilibrium); feeds the S/D `location` factor (demand in discount / supply in premium supports; the reverse opposes; no confirmed range, outside it, or exactly at equilibrium ⇒ unavailable). `sdDetector` version stays 1.
- New defaults in `priceAction` (rulesVersion changes): `tlLookbackSwings`, `rangeMinAtr`, `breakAtr`, `corrAtr`, `fullImpulseAtr`, `pinWickFrac`, `signalMinRangeAtr`. `failed_breakout` added to the adapter's "touched" states. Names via `paName` ("Rising support line", "Bullish engulfing"). No new colours.
- Harness: prefix-equality on 5 fixtures, range/trendline walks (bull + mirrored) and deep H1; deep M15 with candle signals (prior from the other detectors). 5,000 bars: ~57 ms (M15) / ~59 ms (M1), signals dominate (detector only).
